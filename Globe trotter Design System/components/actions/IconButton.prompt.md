Icon-only button; use "raised" for controls floating over the globe (recentrer, calques), "ghost" in headers.
```jsx
<IconButton icon="locate-fixed" label="Recentrer" variant="raised" />
<IconButton icon="x" label="Fermer" />
```
- variant: ghost (default) · outline · raised (round, shadow-2)
- active renders ink fill (toggled tool)
